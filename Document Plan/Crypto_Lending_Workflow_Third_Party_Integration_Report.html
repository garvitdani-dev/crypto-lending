01 / Proposed product

Use the LendFoundry lending journey to originate and service loans secured by crypto

The customer applies for liquidity and pledges a supported digital asset. LendFoundry manages the application, verifications, decision, offer, agreement, funding gate and servicing. Approved partners verify the customer, screen the asset, safeguard collateral, provide prices and move funds.

Existing capabilityProposed crypto capabilityDecision required

What remains familiar

Application, eligibility, program qualification, offer, e-signature, funding, repayment and closure.

What crypto adds

Wallet/custody setup, asset eligibility, provenance screening, valuation, LTV, deposit confirmation and continuous collateral health.

What must be decided

Customer, states, lender, custody model, assets, funding currency, thresholds, pricing and sale process.

Recommended first design: one U.S. customer segment, Bitcoin collateral, USD funding and qualified third-party custody. This is a proposal to validate—not a statement that the legal or commercial model is already approved.
02 / Evidence from the current prototype

Reuse the proven lending skeleton; add crypto-specific evidence and controls

The existing project contains workflow coverage for borrower onboarding, state/program/FICO qualification, DTI recalculation, employed/self-employed/retired income verification, preliminary offers, offer approval and acceptance, Plaid bank verification, documents and funding. These are reusable orchestration patterns, not proof that crypto functions already exist.

Existing LendFoundry behaviorHow it is reusedCrypto extension required
Borrower application and consentRetain application identity, amount, state and product context.Add collateral asset, network, quantity and custody/funding preference.
Program, state and FICO mappingRetain configurable product qualification and reason codes.Add asset/network eligibility, LTV, liquidity and exposure criteria.

04 / Verification and integration map

Third-party participation at every stage

A provider supplies evidence or executes a controlled action. LendFoundry still owns orchestration, policy application, reason codes, cases and the complete loan record. Provider names are evaluation options—not final selections.

Stages1 · Application
Verification / actionConsent, duplicate and completeness checks.
System ownerLendFoundry workflow and customer profile.
Third-party optionsNormally none; communication provider may deliver OTP/email. Evidence: consent version, time and application ID.
Stage2 · Identity
Verification / actionIdentity attributes, government ID, document authenticity, selfie/liveness and address as required.
System ownerLendFoundry applies pass/review/fail policy and manages exceptions.
Third-party optionsSocure · Persona · Alloy · Trulioo. Plaid may support bank-linked identity but is not a replacement for every KYC need.
Stage3 · AML / sanctions
Verification / actionSanctions, PEP/adverse-media and jurisdiction/product restrictions.
System ownerCompliance policy, review queue and decision evidence remain internal.
Third-party optionsComplyAdvantage · LSEG World-Check · LexisNexis. Evidence: query, result, match details and disposition.
Stage4 · Wallet / custody
Verification / actionCreate custody account/address or prove control of a self-custody wallet.
System ownerLendFoundry stores the approved reference and never substitutes an unverified address.
Third-party optionsBitGo · Coinbase Custody · Anchorage Digital; Fireblocks ecosystem. Wallet-signature service for non-custodial design.
Stage5 · Asset eligibility
Verification / actionAsset, network, token contract, custody support and liquidity policy.
System ownerInternal asset master and lender-approved risk parameters.
Third-party optionsCustodian asset catalogue; institutional market/liquidity data. No provider alone decides eligibility.
Stage6 · Balance / provenance
Verification / actionAvailable quantity, transaction history and illicit-risk exposure.
System ownerLendFoundry reconciles expected and observed quantity and opens review cases.
Third-party optionsBitGo/custodian · Alchemy/Infura/QuickNode · Chainalysis/TRM Labs/Elliptic.
Stage7 · Price
Verification / actionApproved price, time, freshness, deviation and fallback.
System ownerInternal price policy chooses usable observation and haircut.
Third-party optionsInstitutional market-data vendor; Chainlink or Pyth when an on-chain oracle is justified.
Stages8–9 · Risk / program
Verification / actionLTV, exposure, concentration, borrower rules and program match.
System ownerLendFoundry risk and program engines with lender-approved configuration.
Third-party optionsTransUnion/Experian/Equifax only if credit policy requires; Plaid/MX/Argyle for income or cash flow if required.
Stages10–11 · Offer / e-sign
Verification / actionVersioned terms, acceptance, identity of signer and signed evidence.
System ownerLendFoundry creates the offer/document package and freezes material terms.
Third-party optionsDocuSign · Adobe Acrobat Sign · Dropbox Sign; notification provider for delivery.
Stages12–13 · Deposit / confirmation
Verification / actionCorrect destination, asset, network, quantity, confirmations and control state.
System ownerCrypto-services adapter plus LendFoundry funding gate and reconciliation.
Third-party optionsBitGo/custodian · blockchain RPC/node · analytics rescreening · price provider.
Stage14 · Funding
Verification / actionApproved account ownership, single disbursement, settlement, return/failure.
System ownerLendFoundry creates one funding order and tracks final status.
Third-party optionsSponsor bank / approved payment provider; Plaid for account linking/ownership where applicable.
Stages15–16 · Servicing / monitoring
Verification / actionBalance, interest, payments, collateral quantity, price, LTV and alert delivery.
System ownerLendFoundry servicing and cases; collateral monitor produces risk events.
Third-party optionsPrice provider/oracle · custodian · RPC · Twilio/SendGrid/existing communication service.
Stages17–18 · Repay / release
Verification / actionCleared repayment, no holds, approved destination, one release and final reconciliation.
System ownerLendFoundry authorizes closure only when debt and collateral records agree.
Third-party optionsBank/payment rail or approved digital-asset rail · custodian/BitGo · RPC · wallet screening.

06 / Servicing, health and liquidation

The collateral must be managed for the entire loan

Healthy

Payments and collateral remain within policy. Continue monitoring.

Warning

Notify and offer top-up or partial repayment.

Action

Restrict activity and require the approved cure.

Sale eligible

Validate price/authority and begin the controlled process.

Liquidation is not a UI status. It requires trusted price evidence, authority to act, a sale venue, customer notices, execution, debt application, fee/surplus/shortfall accounting and reconciliation.
EventSystem responseThird-party dependency
Price changesRevalue collateral and recalculate LTV/health.Price provider/oracle plus custody balance.
Warning thresholdCreate event, notify customer and expose cure actions.Communication provider; custody/payment paths for cure.
Sale thresholdValidate price, hold status and authority; execute permitted sale.Custodian, approved sale venue, price and blockchain evidence.
Payment postedApply under contract, update debt and recalculate LTV.Bank/payment settlement evidence.
Full payoffConfirm cleared funds/no holds; release remaining asset once.Custodian/RPC and destination screening.
